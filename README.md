## 📖 About

🔹 **Terraria — Wikithis** is a powerful tool built to help you look up Terraria wiki information for any item, enemy, or object directly from within the game. Hover over any item or press a hotkey to open the official Terraria wiki page for that subject in your browser.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Terraria — Wikithis** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their Terraria modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Hotkey Wiki Lookup | Press a configured key to open the wiki page for the hovered item |
| Context Menu | Right-click any item in inventory for a direct wiki link option |
| Enemy Pages | Clicking enemies in the bestiary opens their detailed wiki entry |
| Mod Support | Can link to CurseForge or mod wiki pages for modded items |
